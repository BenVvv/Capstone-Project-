## Project Overview

In this project, I take on the role of a **Data Analyst** for **OZ Bikes**, a fictional bike rental company that experienced significant business disruptions during the COVID-19 lockdowns. The objective of this analysis is to support strategic planning and operational readiness as restrictions are lifted.

Using data-driven techniques, I explore customer demand patterns and identify the key factors that influence bike rental activity. The insights generated help forecast future demand and determine which variables have the strongest impact on rental volume, enabling the business to make informed decisions that maximize value, improve resource allocation, and support post-pandemic recovery.
